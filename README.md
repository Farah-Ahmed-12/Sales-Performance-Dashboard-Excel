Interactive **Excel dashboard** analyzing **4 years of retail sales data (2014–2017)** — covering **9,994 transactions, 5,009 orders, 793 customers, and 1,862 products** — to uncover profitability drivers, discount impact, customer behavior, shipping performance, and return patterns.

##  Project Overview
Built an end-to-end reporting solution in Excel, including **data cleaning, calculated fields, PivotTables, PivotCharts, and an interactive KPI dashboard** to answer business questions around customer loyalty, profitability, shipping, returns, and regional performance.

##  Key Insights

1. **Discount Impact:** Orders with no discount generated **$321K in profit**, while orders discounted above 20% generated a combined **$135K loss**. All loss-making line items had a discount applied.
2. **Category Profitability:** **Technology** generated $145K profit on $836K in sales, while Furniture generated only $18K profit on $742K in sales. **Tables** was the weakest sub-category, generating a **$17.7K loss** on $207K in sales.
3. **Geographic Profit Concentration:** **California and New York generated 65% of total profit ($150K)**, while Texas, Ohio, Pennsylvania, and Illinois recorded a combined **$71K loss**.
4. **Seasonality:** **Q4 (September–December)** generated approximately **40% of annual revenue**. February was the weakest month at approximately **$60K**, compared with **$352K in November**.
5. **Shipping Performance:** **Standard Class** accounted for approximately **60% of orders**, making it the most widely used shipping mode. All shipping modes remained profitable, while Same Day represented approximately **5% of order volume**.
6. **Returns:** Approximately **8% of orders were returned**, representing around **$180K in sales value**, with **Binders and Paper** among the leading returned categories.
7. **Customer Profitability:** The highest-revenue customer, **Sean Miller ($25K revenue)**, generated a **$2K loss**, showing that high revenue does not necessarily translate into high customer profitability.
